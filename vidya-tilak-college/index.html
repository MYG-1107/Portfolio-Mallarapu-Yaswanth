Project overview

The repository describes a public website for Vidya Tilak College of Management Foundation. It includes programme information and guidance for regular, online, distance, undergraduate, postgraduate, doctoral and research pathways.

The problem it addresses

Prospective students need clear, well-organised information about eligibility, admissions steps, documentation, finance, programme choices and student support.

Engineering approach

The repository uses a Jekyll-style layout with shared includes, layouts, structured guide content and many dedicated information pages. The README records additional search-focused guides, article metadata and structured data for educational content and events.

Key capabilities

  • Admissions process, checklist, eligibility and application guidance.
  • UG, PG, PhD, online and distance programme information.
  • Career pathways, scholarships, student support and FAQs.
  • Blog and guide content with topic tags and structured metadata.
  • Dedicated institutional, research and collaboration information.

Scope and limitations

Admissions requirements, university recognition, course availability, fees and deadlines are time-sensitive. Visitors should verify current details directly with the institution and relevant regulators.
