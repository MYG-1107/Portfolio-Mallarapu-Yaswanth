Head Exercise Application

A browser-based movement counter with optional camera-assisted direction estimates and a manual counting mode.

Project overview

The Head Exercise Application counts completed movement cycles in either camera mode or manual mode. The user chooses whether to grant camera permission; the application does not require an account.

The problem it addresses

A lightweight utility should remain usable for people who prefer not to grant camera access or whose device/browser does not support the tracking capability.

Engineering approach

The application shell coordinates camera permission, calibration, timers, prompts and CSV export. A separate framework-free tracker-core module handles direction classification and completed move/return-to-centre cycles so the counting logic can be tested independently.

Key capabilities

  • Camera mode with user-triggered permission and neutral-position calibration.
  • Manual mode that works without camera access.
  • Movement/return-to-centre cycle counting, timer and progress updates.
  • Pause, stop, reset and user-triggered CSV summary export.
  • Keyboard/accessibility settings and limited offline shell caching for manual mode.

Scope and limitations

This is a movement-counting utility, not a medical device, diagnosis, clinical range-of-motion assessment, exercise prescription or physiotherapy plan. Camera direction is an estimate. Camera frames are processed in the browser according to the repository design; a real-device review is still needed for camera behaviour.
