Project overview

The repository is a static professional profile website for Dr. Mujahid Tabassum. Its page content presents his academic and professional background, research interests, qualifications, experience and professional contacts.

Project objectives

A long academic and engineering career can be difficult to communicate through a short biography. A structured professional website helps organise experience, research interests, academic profiles and credentials into one public reference.

Implementation approach

The repository contains a semantic HTML page with dedicated About, Experience, Education, Skills, Research, Awards and Contact sections, plus separate stylesheet and JavaScript files. The page includes a mobile navigation control and metadata for search and sharing.

Website sections and capabilities

  • Professional introduction and quick-profile section.
  • Research interests and linked academic-profile references.
  • Detailed work experience and academic qualifications.
  • Skills and research/awards sections.
  • Contact section and responsive navigation.

Scope and attribution

This website presents the professional profile of Dr. Mujahid Tabassum. The profile subject is not the owner of this portfolio. Role titles, qualifications and other biographical statements belong to the published profile and should be verified with the subject or institution.
