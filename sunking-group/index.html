Project overview

The repository title and live domain identify SUNKING GROUP as an education, training and consulting website. Its page structure includes about, education, leadership, consulting, programmes, training, contact and policy content.

The problem it addresses

An organisation with multiple service areas needs a consistent public information structure so visitors can understand its work and reach the right section.

Engineering approach

The repository contains shared includes, individual HTML entry pages and clean directory routes for the primary site sections, together with branding, favicon, robots and sitemap files.

Key capabilities

  • About and organisational information.
  • Education and programme information.
  • Leadership and training sections.
  • Consulting and contact pages.
  • Shared assets, navigation includes and sitemap/robots configuration.

Scope and limitations

The site describes organisational services. Programme availability, accreditation, fees and service terms should be confirmed with SUNKING GROUP directly.
