Sri Maha Durga Shakti Sena — Devotional Video Playlist

A devotional playlist experience that brings YouTube-hosted songs and bhajans into one organised place.

  • Media & community

Project overview

The devi repository describes a central place to access devotional songs and bhajans dedicated to Goddess Kanaka Durga and other deities. The experience is designed around discovery and playback of material that remains hosted on YouTube.

The problem it addresses

Listeners often have to search across separate channels and playlists to find the devotional tracks they want. A single organised interface can reduce that friction while retaining the original hosting source.

Engineering approach

The repository README describes YouTube API integration as the source of the playlist tracks. The page groups video resources for browsing and playback rather than redistributing the underlying audio files.

Key capabilities

  • A central playlist-style entry point for devotional songs and bhajans.
  • YouTube-sourced playback and links to the original hosted media.
  • A lightweight browser experience without requiring a separate media backend.

Scope and limitations

Tracks are sourced from YouTube. Their availability, playback and rights remain subject to YouTube and the original content owners; this project does not claim ownership of the songs or recordings.
