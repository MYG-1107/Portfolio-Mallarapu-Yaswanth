My Dream Home — Interactive 3D House Visualizer

A Three.js model of a north-facing, three-level home that visitors can inspect floor by floor and room by room.

  • Project case study
  • 3D & interactive visualization
View GitHub repository ↗All projects

Project overview

The repository describes a compact, north-facing Indian home concept with a ground-floor utility and cattle-shed layout, a second-floor 3BHK plan and a third-floor 2BHK plan.

The problem it addresses

A conventional static floor plan can make it difficult to understand how rooms and levels relate spatially. An interactive 3D model lets visitors orbit the scene and inspect individual floors.

Engineering approach

Three.js renders the home as an interactive scene. OrbitControls support mouse/touch exploration, and the page provides floor selection, room chips, floor-plan and roof visibility, room-tour, rotation and camera-reset controls.

Key capabilities

  • Inspect all floors together or isolate the ground, second or third floor.
  • Orbit and zoom the model, or move the camera to a selected room.
  • Toggle the floor plan, roof, room tour and rotation.
  • Explore the north-facing entry, shared stair core, residential rooms and utility layout.
  • Responsive controls for desktop and mobile screens.

Scope and limitations

This is an illustrative 3D concept, not a construction drawing. Dimensions, structural design, animal capacity, ventilation, drainage, roof loads, setbacks and approvals require review by qualified local professionals against a surveyed plot and current regulations.
