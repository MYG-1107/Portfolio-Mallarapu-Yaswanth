Project overview

The GitHub profile includes several repositories whose page titles identify them as professional-profile websites. Grouping them here makes the collection easier to browse without suggesting that the people featured on those pages are the developers.

The problem it addresses

A professional profile site needs to organise an individual's biography, roles, education, research, publications, awards and contact information into an accessible public reference.

Engineering approach

The public repositories use individual static-site layouts and content structures. Their titles and canonical URLs identify the profile subjects and live page destinations.

Key capabilities

  • Dr. Mujahid Tabassum repository: academic, IT consultant and chartered-engineer profile (live website).
  • Dr. Saju Mohanan repository: academic leadership, AI/full-stack development and cybersecurity profile (live website).
  • Prof. Dr. Prakash Divakaran repository: academic leadership and supply-chain expertise (live website).
  • Puppireddy Vishwateja repository: full-stack development and AI engineering portfolio (live website).

Scope and limitations

These links are presented as a collection of repositories and public profile sites. The site subjects are the people named in each repository, not the owner of this portfolio.
