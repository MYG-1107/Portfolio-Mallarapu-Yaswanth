Project overview

The repository contains a static professional website for Dr. Saju Mohanan. Its content is organised around academic and executive experience, certifications, events, technical skills, education, honours, publications and contact information.

Project objectives

A professional with academic leadership responsibilities and technology experience needs a clear way to present multiple areas of work without overwhelming visitors with an unstructured CV.

Implementation approach

The page uses a single structured portfolio layout with dedicated sections for work history, certifications, events, skills, education, awards, research publications and professional contacts. The markup includes mobile navigation controls and section anchors.

Website sections and capabilities

  • Professional overview and executive experience timeline.
  • Certifications and events organised/chaired.
  • Key skills and technical competencies.
  • Education background, awards and research publications.
  • Contact information and academic-profile links.

Scope and attribution

This site presents Dr. Saju Mohanan's profile; it is not a biography of the owner of this portfolio. The page content is reproduced as a website-project summary, not independent verification of the profile's statements.
